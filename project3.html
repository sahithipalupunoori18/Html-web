Vasavi College Of Engineering

Vasavi College of Engineering, located in Ibrahimbagh, Hyderabad, is an autonomous private engineering institution affiliated with Osmania University. Established in 1981 by the Vasavi Academy of Education, it is accredited by NAAC with an A++ grade and offers robust undergraduate and postgraduate engineering programs.

Key details & Academics

  • Location: Ibrahimbagh, Hyderabad, Telangana (near the historic Golconda Fort).
  • Affiliation & Status: Autonomous institution affiliated with Osmania University and approved by AICTE.
  • Programs Offered: Offers Under-Graduate (B.E/B.Tech) courses in Civil, Mechanical, ECE, CSE, EEE, IT, and CSE (AI & ML), alongside Post-Graduate (M.E/M.Tech and MCA) programs.
  • Admissions: Primarily through state-level entrance exams like TG EAPCET and TG PGECET, as well as JEE Main and GATE scores.
